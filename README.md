# Hi, I'm Jongha Kim 👋

M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Sogang University, advised by Prof. Hyung-Min Park in the Intelligent Information Processing Lab (IIP).

I work on **lightweight and robust automatic speech recognition (ASR)**, connecting ASR with language models, and **large audio-language models (LALMs)** that understand spatial and acoustic information.

### Selected projects

- [**Medi_Voxtral_finetuning**](https://github.com/hytric/Medi_Voxtral_finetuning): medical ASR and spoken QA with Voxtral Mini 3B and QLoRA
- [**Korean_Audio_Multilingual_HuBERT_Translation**](https://github.com/hytric/Korean_Audio_Multilingual_HuBERT_Translation): textless Korean–English speech-to-speech translation
- [**awesome-speech-ai**](https://github.com/hytric/awesome-speech-ai): notes and paper summaries in speech AI
- [**music-source-separation**](https://github.com/hytric/music-source-separation): vocal and instrumental separation demo with CLI and Docker
